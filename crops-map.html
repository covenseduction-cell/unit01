Crops map

Where each crop will grow in the Caeldun Isles. Potato and beetroot grow everywhere; pumpkins only on the Spiderholme Isles.

Each colour shows arable ground where that crop will grow. Tick crops on and off below the map.

Crop summary

CropWhere it growsArable land
PotatoEverywhere, at full speed82.0%
BeetrootEverywhere, at full speed82.0%
WheatThe Breadbasket at full speed; the Rainlands at 1% speed31.3%
CarrotThe Breadbasket only3.2%
PumpkinThe Spiderholme Isles only: Ruannis, Maigor and Cannoch1.9%
MelonThe Breadbasket only3.2%
Sugar caneThe Breadbasket only, right beside water0.4%

"Arable land" is the share of all land where that crop can be farmed. Most of the wheat country on the map is Rainlands ground, where wheat grows at a hundredth of normal speed. Only a small share of the world grows grain properly.

These rules are enforced in game: a crop won't grow outside the lands listed here, and bone meal won't help. See Farming & crops.

Settlers III is a community Minecraft server. This wiki describes how the server's own plugins work; numbers come from the live configuration and can change as the world is tuned.
