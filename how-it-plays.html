How Settlers plays

The rules that make Settlers III different from vanilla survival, on one page.

If you've played a lot of vanilla survival, most of your instincts still work. These are the things that don't.

The idea behind it

Slow progress is the point. In most servers the first week is a race to diamond gear, elytra and farms, and then there's nothing to do but wait. Here, distance is real and scarcity is regional, so a settlement's wealth depends on where it is, what it can make, and who it trades with.

That's meant to produce politics on its own. Whoever controls the only good farmland feeds the world. Whoever holds the iron country equips it. The sea lanes between them matter. You don't need to roleplay any of this; it follows from the rules.

What you keep

  • Your inventory on death. Dying costs time and a little nutrition, not your belongings.
  • Vanilla crafting, with a few adjustments (rails craft 32 at a time, for example).
  • Vanilla combat, against a very different set of creatures.

Settlers III is a community Minecraft server. This wiki describes how the server's own plugins work; numbers come from the live configuration and can change as the world is tuned.
