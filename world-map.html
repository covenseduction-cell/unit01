Maps

World map

The whole of the Caeldun Isles: every region, and what each one is known for.

The map covers the whole world, 32,768 × 45,056 blocks. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and read the block coordinates under your cursor in the corner. Each region is labelled with the resources its land is known for; hover an icon to see its name.

Reading the map

  • Colour on land is height: green lowland, tan hills, pale rock and snow on the highest summits.
  • Pale sea near the coast is the shallow shelf, safe for swimming and small boats. Darker blue offshore is the abyss. See The open ocean.
  • Purple lines are the borders between regions.
  • The dark red island in the far north is the Burning Isle.
