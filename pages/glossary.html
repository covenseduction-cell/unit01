TermMeaning
AbyssThe deep ocean beyond an island's shelf, more than 40 blocks deep. Deadly to swimmers and small boats. See The open ocean.
ApprenticeThe rank every new player starts with.
BlaeberryScots for bilberry, a low shrub of the pinewood floor.
BurnA Highland stream. Burns with gravel beds are where you pan.
Caeldun IslesThe world of Settlers III.
CragA rocky outcrop.
DistrictA rich patch of an ore within its home land. See Resources.
EncumbranceBeing slowed down by what you carry. See Weight.
GlenA long, deep Highland valley.
HangHow far below itself a material can hold something up. See Gravity.
Helm / wheelThe block you steer a ship from.
KeeperAn NPC that runs a player's shop.
LandOne of five groups of regions sharing a climate, ores and crops: Outer Isles, Northlands, Spiderholme, Breadbasket, Rainlands.
LochA lake, or a sea inlet.
MachairFlat, flowery, sandy grassland behind the beaches of the western isles.
RamsonsWild garlic, carpeting the oakwood floor in spring.
ReachHow far sideways a material can carry a load. See Gravity.
RegionOne of the fifteen named areas of the map, each based on a real island or stretch of coast.
ShelfThe shallow sea around an island, safe for swimming and small boats.
ShingleA beach of pebbles and gravel. Most Caeldun beaches are shingle; sand is found in sheltered bays.
SpellA stretch of weather lasting 10 to 40 minutes, either wet or bright. See Weather.
