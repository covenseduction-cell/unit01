Vanilla's zombies, skeletons and creepers don't roam the Caeldun Isles. Each region has its own roster of creatures that fits the land, the climate and the height. Some are dangerous, some are dinner, and some are just birdsong.

Accept the resource pack

Many creatures are repainted by the server's resource pack. Without it, a red deer looks like a camel and a mosquito looks like a bee.

A creature's name only shows when you look straight at it.

Spirits of the high ground

The higher you climb, the more likely you are to meet things that aren't animals. They're peaceful until provoked.

SpiritNotes
Forest spriteA small green spirit that throws magick when attacked. Often carries a gold nugget.
Wandering soulA lost skeleton, unarmed and unarmoured, that hits very hard once angered. Becomes steadily more common above the treeline.
Soul riderA wandering soul on a spectral steed. Rarer, and found higher still.

The Breadbasket

Tirwenn and Colvane are too small for large animals. There are no bears, wolves or deer, and no hostile humanoids at all. Being warm, they're a breeding ground for smaller things.
