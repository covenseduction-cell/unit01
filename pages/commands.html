Getting around

There are no teleport commands: no /home, /spawn, /tpa, /back or warps. You get around on foot, on horseback, by rail or by ship. See Banned & changed.

Survival

CommandWhat it does
/statsShow your protein, fruit and veg, grains and thirst. See Nutrition.
/needs hud on|offShow or hide the needs bar.

Ships

CommandWhat it does
/ship create <name>Make the block you're looking at a ship's wheel.
/ship menuThe wheel menu for the ship you're on.
/ship boardJoin a ship's crew.
/ship leaveLeave the crew.
/ship dockDrop anchor.
/ship listYour ships and where they are.
/ship removeStop a build being a ship (the blocks stay).
/shipHelp.

At sea: W/S throttle, A/D steer, sneak twice to get off. See Ships.

Shops

CommandWhat it does
/shop create <name>Open a shop where you stand.
/shop listYour shops.
/shop <name>Stock, gold and owners.
/shop <name> add|remove <player>Manage co-owners.
/shop <name> moveMove the keeper to you.
/shop <name> rename <new>Rename the shop.
/shop <name> skinChange the keeper's look.
/shop <name> deleteClose the shop (empty it first).
/shop helpHelp.

See Gold & shops.

CommandWhat it does

Handy key moves

ActionDoes
Sneak + dropThrow away your heaviest stack (for when you're sinking). See Weight.
Sneak while choppingTake one log instead of felling the whole tree. See Felling.
Right-click water with a bowlPan for gold. See Panning.
Right-click a ship's wheelOpen the helm menu.
Right-click a shop keeperTrade.
