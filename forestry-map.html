Forestry map

Which trees grow where across the Caeldun Isles, and how thick the woods are.

Each colour is one kind of tree. The stronger the colour, the more trees per chunk; a full wood is about 7 per chunk. Tick species on and off below the map.

"Found on" is the share of all land where that tree grows at all.

What it means

  • The Outer Isles have almost no trees. Settlers there import timber, or build in stone and turf.
  • Timber is the best structural material for its reach (see Gravity), so the wooded lands have a builder's advantage.
  • Saplings grow into the region's own tree shapes. See Tree felling.

Settlers III is a community Minecraft server. This wiki describes how the server's own plugins work; numbers come from the live configuration and can change as the world is tuned.
